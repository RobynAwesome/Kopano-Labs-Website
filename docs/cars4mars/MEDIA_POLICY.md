# Cars4Mars Media Policy

Campaign art and generated astronaut media are visual storytelling assets. They are never physical rover evidence.

Rules:
- Mission Control is the source of truth.
- Campaign/generated media must be labelled as campaign or concept media.
- Physical evidence requires dated build/test records.
- Lite and Save-Data modes prefer static imagery; motion is optional enhancement.
- A broken video must degrade to the local poster and canonical channel link, never to an empty box.
