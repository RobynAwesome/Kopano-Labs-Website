# AUGUST 2026 — ESTATE EXECUTION DIRECTIVE

Owner directive. Applies to Kopano Labs public/product work throughout August 2026.

## Objective

Make the estate **work, connect, update, and become visibly current** so the strongest MVPs attract the right users, datasets, collaborators, institutions and investors before the Kopano Labs AI product is deployed in the next 6–12 months.

## Public website rule

KopanoLabs.com is no longer primarily an explainer of what the business is. The public surface must show **what we are doing now**.

Prefer, in order:

1. working interactive product;
2. live or recorded video;
3. image / screenshot / evidence media;
4. 3D / spatial object;
5. concise state or backing data;
6. prose only where the above cannot carry the meaning.

Do not use five paragraphs where one visual can carry the state.

## FOC validation rule

Every MVP must expose separate signals for:

- WORKS — does the product/function actually operate now?
- VISIBLE — can the relevant public/audience inspect it?
- BACKING — is there verified external institutional/commercial backing?
- CURRENT — is the public state up to date?
- CONNECTED — does it feed the wider Kopano Labs evidence/data/product estate?

FOC groups currently owner-validated for implementation:

- **Fake of Concept** — presentation or claim outruns current evidence/state.
- **Freedom of Concept** — agency exists, but actions remain governed by consequences, evidence and ownership.

Do not invent additional FOC groups. Add them only when owner definitions are available.

## Toolchain rule

For repositories that use TypeScript, **TypeScript 7 is the target toolchain**. Do not downgrade to TypeScript 5/6 merely for familiarity. Upgrade dependencies/configuration and repair compatibility issues against TS7.

Also audit and update stale libraries/dependencies during active repository work. Do not mass-upgrade blindly without a build/runtime receipt.

## Block rule

Before allowing any blocker to stop delivery, state:

1. exactly what failed;
2. why it matters;
3. whether the requirement is still required;
4. what work can continue without it;
5. the materially different workaround/implementation path.

If the requirement is obsolete, retract it instead of repeatedly repairing it.

## Estate rule

A repository existing is not success. An MVP existing is not success.

The monthly target is:

`WORKING → CONNECTED → CURRENT → VISIBLE → EVIDENCED → BACKABLE`

Public claims and visual states must not outrun evidence.

## AI dependency

The future Kopano Labs AI product is not treated as isolated. The quality of the surrounding products, datasets, telemetry, users, evidence, connectors and institutional backing is part of its launch readiness.
